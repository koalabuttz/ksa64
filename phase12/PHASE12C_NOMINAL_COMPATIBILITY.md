# Phase 12C nominal-display compatibility decision

Status: accepted implementation constraint.

## Decision

Phase 12C preserves two distinct Phase 10 nominal lineages:

1. **Frozen Phase 10 evidence** supplies the labelled planned/reference path.
   Its checked-in KTT10, KPH10, and KSR10 bytes are immutable.
2. **Current portable re-execution** supplies exact-release SIM-truth display
   samples. It uses the checked-in Phase 10 implementation and fixtures without
   changing physics.

The viewer is exposed only after a fail-closed audit accepts both lineages.
Render products carry different model/evidence identities, so a renderer cannot
present current re-execution as if it were the frozen artifact.

## Why this exception exists

During Phase 12C exact-replay work, fresh debug and release builds produced the
same deterministic artifacts, but those artifacts did not reproduce the frozen
Phase 10 hashes after recovery began. Rebuilding the Phase 10 completion source
at commit `449746b` produced the same current lineage, showing that the
disagreement predates Phase 12C rather than being a later physics change.

The first KTT10 byte difference is offset `124720`, inside the checksum region
of sparse frame step `15328`. The first physical-state difference follows at
step `15424`. Frame ownership, segment, event, command, alarm, attitude,
angular-rate, and mass schedules remain exact.

Changing current physics merely to reproduce stale bytes would hide the
evidence problem. Replacing the frozen artifacts would violate the project's
compatibility rule. Phase 12C therefore changes neither.

## Display labelling

- `ACCEPTED_NOMINAL_REFERENCE_MODEL_ID` identifies the frozen planned path.
- `CURRENT_NOMINAL_REEXECUTION_MODEL_ID` identifies exact-release SIM truth
  regenerated by the current portable implementation.
- SIM truth remains available only to the SIM Director, is hidden by default,
  and retains the persistent `SIM TRUTH` label.

This is a presentation compatibility exception, not a new canonical artifact,
physics profile, mission result, or permission to tolerate future drift.
